Lodging


Accommodation for all participants will be provided at Hotel Turia Granada. This includes all meals (breakfast, lunch and dinner) during the workshop period, except on Sunday 1 June, Wednesday evening (due to the special workshop dinner) and Friday evening. Travel information on how to arrive to Granada and IMAG is available on the IMAG website.
